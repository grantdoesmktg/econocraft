## What exists and works
- **Start:** a 5x5x3 grass island with a spruce tree in the void dimension `economy_core:isles` ("The Isles").
  - Skyblock Builder uses spawnDimension isles and forceSkyblockCheck.
  - The welcome quest's checkmark gives the starter kit: Time in a Bottle, Market Crate and Supply Market.
- **Frontier:** the real overworld. It's bought at tier 3 through the gate quest, then reached with the Frontier
  Gateway block. Landing uses generator.getBaseHeight, which avoids a chunk-gen freeze. Dimensions are locked
  by ProgressiveStages.
- **Market Crate:** the sell market.
  - Prices drop as you sell more of one thing (diminishing returns) and recover when you sell a variety.
  - Coin withdraw has a coin picker; you can put coins back in.
  - Auto-sell runs every 10 ticks in chunks of 2 << min(tier, 5): tier 0 sells 2 at a time, then 4, 8, and so on.
  - Sales trigger celebration effects and milestone advancements.
  - Prices come from `config/economy_core/market.json` (130 items, 13 categories), generated by tools/market_catalog.py and shipped by packwiz since 2026-10-04.
- **Supply Market:** a two-tall shop block using FfB models.
  - Tier tabs 0-6, a Machines/Supplies toggle, search, and a detail panel.
  - It sells only the entry machine of each family; upgrades are crafted.
  - Buying pays from the team balance. Bought machines get a price tag so they sell back at 90/85/80% of cost,
    depending on machine tier.
  - The catalog is `config/economy_core/shop.json`, generated by tools/market_catalog.py.
  - 0.9.3 added tier names (Castaway, Tinkerer, Engineer, Pioneer, Cultivator, Industrialist, Tycoon),
    a blurb for each tier, gate costs on locked shelves, and tab tooltips. The text is in the mod's lang en_us.json.
  - 0.9.4 switched to light text on wood (cream on brown tabs, dark wood detail panel). Grant didn't like dark
    text with drop shadows on tan.
- **BalanceHud:** the team balance, shown bottom-left on the HUD.
- **Multiplayer:** the market account, tier and milestones are shared per FTB team. IslandPartyLink auto-links
  Skyblock Builder islands to FTB parties. Quest progress is shared per team.
- **Tier gates** (lifetime earnings / coin fee handed in at the gate quest):

  | Tier | Lifetime earnings | Coin fee |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | 4,000 | 2,000 |
  | 2 | 24,000 | 10,000 |
  | 3 | 96,000 | 40,000 (buys the Frontier) |
  | 4 | 350,000 | 80,000 |
  | 5 | 1,050,000 | 150,000 |
  | 6 | 2,800,000 | 300,000 |

  Doubled 2026-10-05 (Grant). Gate numbers live in: quest_content.py (gate tuples + texts + tier table),
  market_catalog.py (TIERS, earnings_milestones), pacing_sim.py GATES, and the mod (ShopScreen.GATES,
  MarketConfig default milestones, data/economy_core/advancement/earned/<n>.json). End-goal quest is 8,000,000.

  The same numbers are hardcoded in ShopScreen.GATES, so keep the two in sync if they change.
- **Quest book:** FTB Quests, 34 chapters, 179 quests, generated by tools/build_quests.py from tools/quest_content.py.
  - Groups: progression, mods_island, mods_frontier, mods_endgame and reference, with themes and banners
    (tools/make_banners.py).
  - Text goes in lang/en_us.snbt. Escape `&` as `\&`. Quest IDs must be 16 hex digits starting with 1-7.
- **Locks:** tools/build_locks.py writes the ProgressiveStages rules.toml and kubejs/server_scripts/economy_locks.js.
  - The JS removes recipes, including the Lightman's coin mint, FfB market, sieve gems, ghast tear and botany pot variants.
  - ProgressiveStages JEI integration is disabled because it caused a freeze.
- **Commands:**
  - `/market balance|earnings|price`
  - `/market reload|reset all|celebrate|tier set <n>` (op only; `tier set` also grants the matching stages)
  - `/stage grant <player> <stage>`
  - `/kubejs export debug` dumps recipes, tags and loot to local/kubejs/export, which feeds the analyzer.

## Tools (economy-pack/tools, Python 3)
- `market_catalog.py`: the source of sell prices, machines, supplies and buyback rates. Writes MARKET.md,
  shop.json and market.json. Ingots sell by tag (#c:ingots/...) so modded ingots count.
- `build_locks.py`: stage locks and recipe removals (`SHOP_LIVE=True`).
- `build_quests.py` with `quest_content.py`: the quest book.
- `analyze_progression.py`: reads the KubeJS export and writes PROGRESSION-REPORT.md.
  - Last run: 2 tier skips (ghast tear and blaze rod via loot, accepted), 0 craftable shop machines, 0 gaps,
    2 money loops (noted).
- `make_banners.py` (needs the Pillow venv in the old scratchpad; recreate one if needed), `item_index.py`,
  `verify_mods.py`, `nbt.py`, `make_island.py`.
