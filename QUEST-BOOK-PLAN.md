# Economy Pack: Quest Book Plan

Outline for the FTB Quests book. Structure, quest list, rewards and sample copy. Final wording and visual design come after this outline is approved.

## How the book is organized

Two chapter groups, plus a reference group:

1. **Progression** (the main path). One chapter per market tier, from the first log to Tycoon. Every chapter tells you what to build next, why, and what it pays. It links out to the Mods tabs when a mod matters, but never makes you read them.
2. **Mods** (optional deep dives). One chapter per mod or system. If you already know Mekanism, skip the Mekanism tab; the Progression path only asks for results ("make an Enrichment Chamber"), not lessons.
3. **Reference**. The market, the shop, commands and FAQ. Lookup pages, no progression.

**How the two areas connect:** progression quests that introduce a mod show a small "Mods: Cyclic" link quest pointing at that tab. Mod tabs have no hard requirements from the progression line, except that a mod's items are locked until its tier (ProgressiveStages), and the mod tab says so ("This unlocks at tier 2, Engineer. Patience, grasshopper.").

**Voice:** helpful, light, sometimes a little snarky. A friend who has played too many modpacks and wants you to have fun. Short paragraphs, second person, suggestions rather than orders ("I'd consider...", "Now's a good time to..."). Never more than about five lines of text per quest; details live in the Mods tabs and in JEI.

**Rewards:** mostly coins (Lightman's coin items). Rough rule: a chapter's quests together pay about 15-25% of the next tier's gate fee, so following the book helps but never replaces playing. Gate quests pay nothing; they take coins (the tier fee). Occasional item rewards where they unblock a step (a mesh, a bucket). Mod-tab quests pay small amounts, since they're optional.

**Tiers and gates** (from the plan; lifetime earnings needed / fee handed in as coins):

| Tier | Name | Gate | Target time in tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Castaway | start | ~1 h |
| 1 | Tinkerer | 2,000 / 1,000 | ~2 h |
| 2 | Engineer | 12,000 / 5,000 | ~3 h |
| 3 | Pioneer (buy the Frontier) | 48,000 / 20,000 | ~5 h |
| 4 | Cultivator (Nether) | 175,000 / 40,000 | ~7 h |
| 5 | Industrialist (Twilight Forest, End) | 525,000 / 75,000 | ~9 h |
| 6 | Tycoon | 1,400,000 / 150,000 | 13 h+ |

Money shorthand below: 1g = 1 gold coin = 100; 1e = 1 emerald coin = 1,000.

# Open questions

1. **Coin rewards as items or straight to balance?** Item rewards (coins in your inventory) are simple and work today. A quest reward that adds to the market balance needs a small Economy Core hook.
2. **Gate quests:** the coin hand-in uses an FTB Quests item task that consumes coins. Lifetime earnings checks need a small Economy Core hook (scoreboard check task). Fine to build alongside the shop.
3. **How snarky is too snarky?** The samples above are the ceiling; final copy can dial it down per chapter.
4. **Chapter art and icons:** pick after the outline is approved.
