# Economy Pack: design notes

Not shipped in the pack (listed in .packwizignore).

## Core loop
- Skyblock start (Skyblock Builder). Early game is island automation: Ex Deorum sieves/hammers/barrels,
  Ex Compressum auto-sieves, Cyclic (User block = auto clicker, item collector = vacuum hopper),
  Botany Pots, Create.
- Players build a coin economy (Lightman's Currency) on the island.
- The overworld ("Frontier") is bought with coins via an FTB Quests quest; ProgressiveStages
  gates dimension travel until the stage is granted.

## Progression rules (from Grant, 2026-10-03)
- Mystical Agriculture seeds are locked until the Frontier is bought. Mundane crops
  (potatoes, wheat, etc.) are fine on the island; no Mystical Agriculture before the Frontier.
- Stretch progression. AE2 and Industrial Foregoing are late game and quality of life only:
  available for players who like them, never required for progression.
- ProjectE: decided against it (Grant, 2026-10-03). Do not add.
- Grant wants to explore custom content (our own mods) to accompany the journey.

## Incentives to buy the Frontier (candidates)
- Mystical Agriculture unlock (decided).
- Villagers and trading, structures and loot, netherite, biome-only resources.
- Coin shop pays more for Frontier-only goods. Done 2026-10-04: `frontier` sell category; see HANDOFF.
- Villager trading is a convenience, not income: emerald sells for only 40.

## Technical notes
- Skyblock Builder config has per-dimension `isCustom` toggles (overworld/nether/end), plus `keepMainIsland`
  for the End, a spawn-dimension concept, and optional DimensionPerTeam (needs Infiniverse).
  Resolved: the overworld stays normal (the Frontier) and islands live in the void dimension economy_core:isles.
- ProgressiveStages JEI integration is disabled (it caused a client freeze in creative):
  config/progressivestages/progressivestages.toml, [jei] enabled = false.
- ProgressiveStages stages and rules are generated by tools/build_locks.py (economy tiers 1-6).
- Hidden deps found so far: Squat Grow needs Cloth Config; Cyclic needs FLib. Both are undeclared on
  Modrinth. Always scan jars.

## Decisions 2026-10-04 (Grant)
- Feel: steady, not grindy. Loop = first machine, then scale production and automation, then the next obvious machine,
  then gear up, explore for valuable sell items, and kill bosses for big payouts that buy overpowered automation and mob grinding.
- Time in a Bottle is given at spawn (config/skyblockbuilder/starter_inventory.json5), plus one free Market Crate.
  This rewards banked playtime; accept that it softens gating.
- Twilight Forest bosses are the main reward for going there: high trophy and drop prices (category "bosses", in tools/market_catalog.py).
- Structure loot only matters after the Frontier, but still gate loot tables against tier skips.
- /home, /warp and friends come from FTB Essentials.
- Guide book: an FTB Quests book that walks through every stage (not just the first hour), with side tabs per mod.
- Mod round-out installed: QoL, FTB Chunks map, storage (Sophisticated, Drawers, Tom's, Refined), building and tools
  (Mekanism Tools paxels, gadgets, sticks, TiaB), power (Powah, Create addons, Flux, Extreme Reactors, Draconic, JDT),
  farming and food, Nether/End and structures. Skipped: magic, extra dimensions, Ender IO, Immersive Engineering,
  Productive Bees, Incendium (clashes with Amplified Nether), Corpse (clashes with GraveStone), extra minimaps.

## Multiplayer (2026-10-04)
- Market accounts (balance, lifetime earnings, sold-items set), market tier and milestones are shared per FTB Teams team
  (Economy Core 0.7, TeamHelper). Any team member can open and use any of the team's Market Crates.
- Quest progress is shared per FTB team (FTB Quests default). Quest rewards are team rewards (claimed once per team),
  except the personal starter kit.
- Market Crate is in the Supply Market shop at 250 (tier 0); the Supply Market (our own shop block) replaced FfB's market.
- Done: IslandPartyLink auto-links Skyblock Builder islands with FTB parties.

## Shop rules (2026-10-04, Grant)
- No item may be both buyable and craftable, because that creates sell-back loops. The shop sells the entry machine
  of each family and upgrades are crafted. Hand tools such as sieve meshes and hammers are craft-only.
- Shop UI: tier names, a blurb per tier, light text on wood. See HANDOFF.md for the current status.
