# Mod candidates: pick and choose

Every mod here has a build for MC 1.21.1 NeoForge (checked on Modrinth/CurseForge on 2026-10-04).
Downloads are all-time totals. **Starred** = my recommendation. Notes flag how a mod touches the economy:
anything that creates resources or speeds machines should be a purchase in the shop and priced so it doesn't skip tiers.

Not available for 1.21.1 NeoForge (so not listed): Thermal series, Tinkers' Construct, Botania, Alex's Caves, Alex's Mobs,
Deeper and Darker, Create: Steam 'n' Rails, Simply Jetpacks 2, Construction Wand (Construction Sticks replaces it), Trashcans (Trash Cans replaces it).
